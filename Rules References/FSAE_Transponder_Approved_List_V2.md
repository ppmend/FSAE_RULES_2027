Transponders compatible with the Formula SAE timing system are the MyLaps (previously AMB) models:

TR2 (must be the CAR/BIKE type) X2 (must be the CAR/BIKE type) TranX260 (colored red)

The MX (orange color) or X2 MX transponders used by the Baja SAE events will not work with the Formula SAE timing system.

We will check your transponder in Tech Inspection to verify it is functioning, but you must ensure it is compatible.
