# Formula SAE Technical Bulletin:

# Adding & Mounting Your Car’s RFID Tag

# What’s Changing

- FSAE Timing & Scoring uses passive RFID tags to all cars competing in US competitions
- Any tags used from previous FSAE competitions in the USA will work and should be used again. If you do not have a tag from a past year you may ask for one at Registration.
- Tags will be functionally-checked and linked to your car number at Brake Check
- Equipment at short events will automatically read your tag to identify your car number. Endurance will still use the timing transponder.
- We’re doing this to help reduce errors in the timing process and allow for further automation of short events

# Passive RFID Tag Info

Omni-ID® Exo 800

Download datasheet

This passive RFID tag:

- Has no battery; never
    needs charging
- Will be used this year at all dynamic short events & endurance
- Does NOT replace (or interfere with) your MYLAPS transponder
- Should be used year-after-year for all future US competitions
- Might be used during the stages of Tech and other events in the future

# Mounting Location

Mount to a vertical frame member on the DRIVER’s LEFT SIDE of the vehicle

NOTE: Distance from front of vehicle does NOT affect lap timing

# Notes & Details

- Must be mounted on the driver’s left
    side of the vehicle
- Must be mounted at a height between 305mm and 915mm (12”-36”) above the ground
- Must be mounted “vertically” (+/-30°)
- Must face “outward” 45° (+/-30°) from the front/back centerline of the car (Photo shows tag mounted at 90° as instructed in 2016)
- Must not be covered by conductive (metallic, composite) material
- If mounting to a frame tube is undesirable, can be mounted to any material (metallic, plastic, composite) on the car in the vicinity described
- Mount using sound engineering practices

# Summary

- If you have an RFID tag from a previous US

## competition, use it. If not, pick up your RFID tag at Registration

- Mount it to your car as described in this bulletin

## before entering Mechanical Tech

- Leave it on your car until after Endurance
- Keep it and use it again next year
- You still need a MYLAPS transponder for Endurance
