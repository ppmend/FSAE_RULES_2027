Energy Meter Receptacle Ordering

SAE has partnered with an outside vendor, Playing With Fusion, to manage sales, manufacturing, and distribution of the Energy Meter Receptacle moving forward.

The Energy Meter is designed as two components:

Receptacle – modified Amphenol MSDXLF000F

Energy Meter – modified Amphenol MSDXLM000

Note: The Energy Meter itself will not be available for purchase

Teams now have the option to purchase an additional Energy Meter Receptacle, which can be useful for:

Replacements for damaged or lost units

Having a backup unit for peace of mind

*New Teams: All new teams are required to purchase one Energy Meter Receptacle as part of the mandatory equipment needed to operate a competition vehicle.

Orders can be placed directly through the vendor’s website: Playing with Fusion (https://www.playingwithfusion.com/products/263).

Ordering questions? Contact the vendor using the info on their website.

Questions about design or function? Submit a rules inquiry online using the FSAEonline Rules Inquiry module.
