Electric Vehicle Safety Resources

This page serves as a resource page for student teams competing in Formula SAE Electric regarding safety items of storing, traveling and working with high voltage. Resources may be property of SAE International. Resources may also be intellectual property of another source found using the internet.

SAE Standards (Student teams participating in current year competition can access these standards on SAE Mobilus)

SAE J2289 — Electric-Drive Battery Pack System: Functional Guidelines Battery-pack design, safety, venting, thermal management, storage/shipping and labeling

SAE J2344 — Guidelines for Electric Vehicle Safety Overall EV/HV safety framework

SAE J2950 – Recommended Practices for Shipping Transport and Handling of Automotive-Type Battery System – Lithium Ion

SAE J2990 – Hybrid and EV First and Second Responder Recommended Practice

SAE J3235 – Best Practices for Storage of Lithium-ion Batteries
