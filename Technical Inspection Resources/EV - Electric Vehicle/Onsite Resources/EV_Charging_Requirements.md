**Electric Vehicle Charging**

All charging of accumulators at Formula SAE competitions managed by SAE International in North American must occur in the designated charging area.  Charging may only occur from the provided charging receptacles.  Teams will not be permitted to charge from generators or other electrical sources.

Two types of charging receptacles will be provided.

- Level 1 charging will be available at 120V on a NEMA 5-20 receptacle.  The receptacle will have a 20A circuit breaker.
- Level 2 charging available at 208 or 240V on a J1772 connector.  The receptacle will have a 20A circuit breaker and an EVSE (Electrical Vehicle Supply Equipment).  Teams will need to satisfy the J1772 pilot connection requirements for the EVSE to energize the receptacle.  Teams are encouraged to test this functionality prior to arriving at the competition with an EVSE.  The exact voltage of the level 2 charging (208V vs 240V) will be determined by the organizers and will depend on the available electrical infrastructure at the event site.  Teams will not be allowed to choose their desired level 2 charging voltage.

The exact continuous current at which a circuit breaker trips can be influenced by outside factors such as the temperature around the breaker.  As such, it is typical to limit the load on a breaker to 80% of the rated current.  The exact ambient conditions around the circuit breaker cannot be guaranteed.  Teams should be prepared with the ability to reduce their maximum charge current if the circuit breaker is opening.  Teams will not be allowed to switch to a second circuit with a “cooler” breaker to continue charging or to try to avoid tripping the breaker.

Due to the limited number of charging receptacles, teams will only be allowed to use a single receptacle in the charging area at once.  This includes accessory loads such as computers and power supplies in addition to the charger.
